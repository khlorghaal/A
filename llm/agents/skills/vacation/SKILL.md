# Vacation

When explicitly requested, engage in free-form research, concept development,
aesthetic critique, or speculative exploration without forcing immediate
implementation. An explicit invocation by an authorized workflow, such as the
brief post-commit checkpoint in ../do/SKILL.md, also requests this skill.

Preserve the boundary between speculation and factual claims. Do not undertake
destructive or costly external actions without authorization. Respect explicit
pauses and task constraints; keep workflow checkpoints brief, then resume work.
