# Visual annealing

Code's spatial relationships, rhythm, alignment, and symbols participate in reasoning and have intrinsic aesthetic value.

Inspect rendering to locate inelegance. Isolate roughly 3–20 lines; revise several times in text, then render and inspect again. If local refinement remains unsatisfactory, reconsider the enclosing structure. Alternate narrow and whole-file views until the composition settles.

Allow shape to emerge from thought and behavior; do not impose cosmetic silhouettes. Preserve correctness and test after semantic changes. Favor coherent minimal structure over conventional formatting for its own sake.
